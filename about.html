About pablo's patisserie

Pablo's Patisserie is a playful baking game built around drawing, creativity and delicious-looking bakes.

Every game session gives you a majestic item to draw. Draw your order on the canvas and Pablo's checks your drawing, Complete the order and you can share your creation with the community shelf! yayayay

What can you bake?

The game currently includes cookies, cupcakes, croissants, pies, bread, pretzels, doughnuts and cakes.
