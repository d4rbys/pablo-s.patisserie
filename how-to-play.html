How to Play

  1. start the game!. press Play from the home screen!
  2. look at today's order -above the play button!. Pablo will tell you which item to draw!
  3. draw your bake! Use the brush, colour picker and brush-size controls to do your majestic drawing!
  4. Fix mistakes. Use the eraser, undo, redo or clear buttons whenever you need them.
  5. Name your creation. Add your name and a name for your bake.
  6. Submit it. Pablo checks the drawing, if it matches today's order, your creation can be added to the Community Shelf!yayayaa!
