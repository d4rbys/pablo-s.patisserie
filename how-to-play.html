How to Play

  1. Start the game. Choose Play from the home screen.
  2. See today's order. Pablo will tell you which bake to draw.
  3. Draw your bake. Use the brush, colour picker and brush-size controls to create your drawing.
  4. Fix mistakes. Use the eraser, undo, redo or clear buttons whenever you need them.
  5. Name your creation. Add your name and a name for your bake.
  6. Submit it. Pablo checks the drawing. If it matches today's order, your creation can be added to the Community Shelf.
